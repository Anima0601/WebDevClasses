Chaat - My Favorite Food

Chaat is a popular Indian street food known for its burst of flavors — spicy, tangy, sweet, and crunchy all at once! It's made using ingredients like crispy puris, boiled potatoes, chickpeas, yogurt, chutneys, and spices.

Why I Love Chaat

I love chaat because every bite is packed with different textures and flavors. It's refreshing, satisfying, and always exciting to eat — whether it's pani puri, bhel puri, or dahi puri!
